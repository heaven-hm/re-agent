# Legacy Rights Provenance

Record SHA-256, provenance, version, platform, and support status for every original binary, asset, and dependency. Separately document authorization for analysis, execution, modification, independent porting, patch distribution, and asset redistribution. A discontinued product or locally available binary is not an automatic license to redistribute it. Bind the target identity to the maintenance request and evidence. Treat absent authorization as a blocker for the affected operation; do not invent a permission grant. Keep originals immutable, and do not put proprietary binaries or unrelated sensitive data in public evidence bundles. No permission or compatibility outcome is verified merely by selecting this skill.
